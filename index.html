The Last Watch

Hold one stone tower against monsters from every side. Survive to 15:00, then bring down Archie the Destroyer.

The game

The Last Watch is a survival tower defense game. Waves of enemies come from all sides while you balance economy and weapons to try to make it to the end. Buy armaments across nine damage types, stack upgrades and pacts, hold out through sunset into the night, and face Archie, a giant wolf-dog, when the clock reaches 15:00. With thousands of combinations of armaments, upgrades and pacts, and many challenging unlocks to earn, no two runs play the same.

  • Nearly a hundred towers to unlock, each earned a different way
  • A Daily Challenge with the same seed for everyone, and leaderboards
  • Endless mode after you beat Archie
  • Music made by the battle itself: every kill plays a note
