# EPCM Games

The site at https://epcm-games.github.io: a page for The Last Watch and the `app-ads.txt` that verifies its AdMob ads.

- `index.html`: the home page
- `app-ads.txt`: must stay at the top level of the site. AdMob reads it from the developer website in the Google Play listing.
- The privacy policy lives in the `the-last-watch-privacy` repo: https://epcm-games.github.io/the-last-watch-privacy/
